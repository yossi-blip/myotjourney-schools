Privacy policy

My OT Journey · Last updated October 22, 2026

My OT Journey is a planner and wellness app for occupational therapy students, created with Robin Akselrud, OTD. This page explains what the app stores and who can see it.

What we store

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code. There is no password.
  • What you enter in the app: your planner items, goals, daily check-ins (mood, sleep, water, gratitude, movement, food, self-care), worksheets, coloring, countdowns and fieldwork hours.
  • The days you open the app (the date only, not what you did).

Reminders are scheduled on your phone only. We do not use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your information.

Who can see it

Your entries are private to you. They are stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) and protected so that only your signed-in account can read them.

Schools

Joining a school is optional and always your choice. If you join, your program staff can see only:

  • your name, email and the date you joined,
  • how many days you opened the app, and
  • how many days you completed your daily check-in.

They never see what you wrote or chose: no moods, notes, gratitude, sleep, food, movement, goals, worksheets, coloring, countdowns, fieldwork notes or planner items. They only see days after you joined. You can leave your school at any time in Profile → My school, and they stop seeing you right away.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything in it, email yossi@akselrud.com from the address you sign in with. We will delete it within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about privacy: yossi@akselrud.com.
