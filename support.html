My OT Journey support

A planner and wellness journal for occupational therapy students, created with Robin Akselrud, OTD.

Contact us

Email yossi@akselrud.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Signing in

  • Enter your email and tap Send sign-in link. Type the six-digit code from the email. There's no password.
  • No email? Check your spam folder, wait a minute, and request a new code.

Joining your school

  • Open your school's invite link, or go to Profile → My school and enter the code your program gave you.
  • Your school sees only the days you opened the app and completed your daily check-in, never your answers. You can leave anytime from the same screen.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account → type DELETE → confirm. Your account and everything in it are deleted right away. Can't get into the app? Email us from the address you sign in with and we'll delete it for you.
